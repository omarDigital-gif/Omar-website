Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This page explains what Omar Digital Sales collects on this website, what we don’t collect, and which other companies you meet when you click away from here.

Who we are

Omar Digital Sales is a small shop that sells digital products for Threads and Instagram creators. This site is a storefront. It does not have accounts, a mailing list form, or a members area.

What this site collects

We do not ask for your name, email, or payment details on these pages. If you only browse and decline analytics, we are not running our own tracking on you.

Your browser may still store one small preference:

  • Cookie consent — if you choose Accept or Decline, that choice is saved in your browser as localStorage so we do not ask again on every visit. That value never leaves your device through this site.

Cookies and Google Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you press Accept on the cookie banner. If you press Decline, or if you ignore the banner, Analytics is not loaded.

If you accept, Google may set cookies and collect things like which pages you open, your approximate location, device type, and referral source. We use that to see whether the site is useful — not to sell a list of visitors.

You can change your mind by clearing this site’s data in your browser, then choosing Decline or Accept again.

Google’s own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts so the page looks the same on every device. That request can send your IP address to Google even if you decline Analytics. We do not control Google’s use of that request.

Checkout (Gumroad)

Buy buttons and the free guide open Gumroad. If you purchase or enter an email there, that information goes to Gumroad, not to a form on this site. Payment cards are handled by Gumroad and its processors.

Gumroad’s policy: gumroad.com/privacy.

Other links

The Threads profile link goes to Meta’s Threads app. GitHub Pages (Microsoft) hosts these files and may see standard server logs such as IP address and browser type when the page is served. We do not get a dashboard of those logs from this setup.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run ads on this site.
  • We do not create an account for you here.

Children

These products are for creators building a public audience. This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this page: message @omar_digitalsales on Threads.
